# Finding your way around

The parts of the window that stay the same on every tab: the header, the tab bar and the footer.

## The header

The dark strip across the top shows **Vibe Files** and the name of the open cabinet (or **No cabinet open**). On the right:

- **Help** — opens this Help. The F1 key does the same.
- **Text size** with **A-** and **A+** — makes all text and controls smaller or larger. The current size shows between them as a percentage, such as `110%`. Ctrl and + or Ctrl and - do the same from the keyboard.
- **Light** / **Dark** — switches the color theme. The button names the theme you switch to: in the dark theme it reads **Light**.

The text size and theme are kept on this PC, so each person's PC can be set the way they like it.

## The tab bar

Under the header are the six tabs, always in this order: **Browse**, **Intake**, **Search**, **Viewer**, **Sync**, **Admin**. The tab you are on is underlined.

When files are waiting to be processed or filed, the Intake tab shows a count, for example **Intake (4)**. Nothing pops up; the count is the only signal.

- [Browse](help:browse) — the client tree and documents
- [Intake](help:intake) — filing incoming files
- [Search](help:search) — finding documents by their words
- [Viewer](help:viewer) — reading and working on PDF pages
- [Sync](help:sync) — the B2 cloud copy
- [Admin](help:admin) — firm settings

## The footer

The thin bar at the bottom shows, from left to right:

- How many documents and clients the cabinet holds, for example `1204 documents · 86 clients`.
- The state of text reading (OCR) for Search, such as **Search index up to date**, **OCR: 12 documents to read**, or **OCR paused on this PC**. See [OCR](help:ocr).
- The program version, such as `vibe-files 0.1.2`.

On the right is the cabinet name with a small dot.

## Messages

Most tabs show messages at the top of their main area. A blue note tells you what just happened, such as **Moved 2 documents to Baker Farms (1004) / Income Tax / 2025.**, and has a **Dismiss** button. A red line is an error, and says what was not done and why.

## Panes on Browse

Browse is split into three panes: the client tree on the left, the document grid in the middle, and the details (inspector) on the right. You can drag the edge of the tree pane to make it wider or narrower. See [Browsing the cabinet](help:browse).
