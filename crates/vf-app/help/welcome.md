# Welcome to Vibe Files

What Vibe Files is, how it sits on top of your client folders, and where to read next.

## What it is

Vibe Files is the firm's document cabinet. It works over an ordinary Windows folder tree: one folder per client, such as `Smith, John (SMIT1001)` or `Baker Farms (1004)`, with the folders your cabinet template gives each client (for example `Income Tax/2025` or `Permanent/Setup`).

Vibe Files keeps a catalog of every document in that tree: its doc type, tax year, status, source and tags, its earlier versions, and an audit trail of every change. The catalog is an index. The files themselves stay plain PDF and Office files in plain folders.

> Every file in the cabinet still opens in Explorer, with or without Vibe Files. If you copy a file into a client folder with Explorer, Vibe Files picks it up on its own.

## The six tabs

The tab bar is always in this order:

- **Browse** — the client tree, the documents in the folder you pick, and their details. This is where most work happens. See [Browsing the cabinet](help:browse).
- **Intake** — files arriving from scanners, inbox folders and print capture are matched to clients and filed after you preview them. The tab shows a count, such as **Intake (3)**, when files are waiting. See [Intake](help:intake).
- **Search** — find documents by the words in them. See [Search](help:search).
- **Viewer** — pages of a PDF, with find, annotations, page tools and the ten-key tape. See [the Viewer](help:viewer).
- **Sync** — two-way sync of the cabinet with your firm's own B2 bucket. See [Sync](help:sync).
- **Admin** — doc types, folder templates, retention, trash, audit trail and other firm settings. See [Admin](help:admin).

## Changes you can take back

Every change Vibe Files makes (a move, a rename, a metadata edit, a move to trash) is done as one transaction: the file, the catalog and the audit trail change together or not at all. Most of them can be undone with **Undo last** or Ctrl+Z. See [Undoing changes](help:undo).
