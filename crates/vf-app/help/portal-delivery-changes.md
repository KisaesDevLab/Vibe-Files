# Paper signatures and corrected returns

A client sometimes signs on paper, or a change turns up after they have viewed and paid. **Deliver…** handles both on the same card, without billing the client again. These need Vibe Portal 0.5.8 or later; an older portal says so.

## Signed on paper

The **Letters to sign** and **Form 8879** sections each have **Signed on paper…**. Press it, type a note if you like (*Signed at the office*), and press **Mark signed**. The step is done: the client is no longer asked to sign it online, and the steps locked behind it open (a return locked behind the letters, for example). The card's log and the step's PDF say "Signed on paper (marked by the firm)" with your note.

Put the signed paper copy in the client's folder as you would any document; the portal keeps no copy of it.

For Form 8879 you can still wait for the client to press **I've signed it** and then press **Confirm signed**. **Signed on paper…** is for when they never will.

## A corrected return after the client paid

While the card is open, choose the corrected return and press **Save changes**, then **Send the revised return**. The card shows **Updated**, the return and Form 8879 steps open again, and the fee stays paid: the client sees no new bill. Paste a new eSignature link if UltraTax made one.

When the card is already finished, it opens read-only with two buttons:

- **Revise the return…** (or **Revise the documents…**) unlocks the PDF picker. Choose the corrected PDF and press **Save changes**, then **Send the revised return**. The card opens again on the client's page with the steps the revision reopened; the fee stays paid. **Stop revising** leaves it as it was.
- **Send another** starts a new card for the same kind and year. If the last card's fee was paid, **Fee** starts unticked and the window says so: tick it only to bill the client again.

A cancelled card cannot be revised; send another.
