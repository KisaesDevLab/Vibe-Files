# Searching documents

How to find a document by the words on its pages, its file name or its details, narrow the results with filters, open a result in the Viewer and keep a search for later.

## Search the cabinet

1. Open the **Search** tab.
2. Type in **Search for**: a name, a word from the page, part of a number. Every word you type must appear, in the page text or in the file name.
3. Results appear when you pause typing. Each row shows a small picture of the best matching page, the file name, the client, the year, the doc type, which page matched, and a few words around the match, marked in yellow.

Part of a number finds the whole number, so the last four digits of an account are enough. Words of one or two letters are looked for in file names, or on the pages the longer words found; on their own they do not search page text. Scanned pages can be found too, once text recognition has read them (see [text recognition](help:ocr)).

The line above the results says how many documents were found. Only the first 200 are listed; add a word or a filter if the count says "(first 200)".

## Where to look

Beside the box, choose **Cabinet**, **This client** or **This folder**. The last two use the client or folder selected in [Browse](help:browse); they are greyed out until you select one there. **This folder** also looks in the folders below it.

## Filters

The filters work with or without words in the box:

- **Client**: one client, or **Any client**.
- **Year**: a tax year such as `2025`, or `Permanent` for the permanent file. Leave it empty for any year.
- **Doc type**: one doc type, or **Needs doc type** for documents that have none yet. Archived doc types are still listed, marked **(archived)**, so older documents stay easy to find (see [doc types](help:doc-types)).
- **Status** and **Source**: as set in the document's details.
- **Filed from** and **to**: dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Filed by** and **Tag**.
- **Has SSN**: documents whose text holds something that looks like a Social Security number.

**Clear filters** puts them all back.

## Open a result

Click a result row. The document opens in the [Viewer](help:viewer) at the page that matched, with your search words marked on the page.

## Saved searches

1. Set up the words, scope and filters.
2. Click **Save search…**, type a **Name** (for example `1099s received 2025`) and click **Save**.

The search is listed in Browse under **Saved searches**, for everyone in the firm. Clicking it there runs it again and shows the documents found in the Browse list. To remove one, right-click it and choose **Delete saved search**.

## Near duplicates

**Near-duplicate report** lists pairs of documents whose text is at least 80 % the same but whose files differ, such as a rescan or a printed and a scanned copy. Click either name to open it. **Close report** hides the list.

> If a scanned document cannot be found by a word you can see on the page, check the footer: text recognition may still be reading it.
