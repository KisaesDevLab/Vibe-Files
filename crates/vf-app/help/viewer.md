# Viewing a document

How to open a PDF in the Viewer, move through its pages, zoom, find words, and what **Save**, **Save as…** and **Close** do.

## Opening a document

- In [Browse](help:browse), double-click a PDF, or right-click it and choose **Open**.
- In the Browse preview, click **Open in Viewer** to open at the page shown.
- On the [Search](help:search) tab, click a result: it opens at the page that matched, with your words marked.

Only PDFs open in the Viewer. Other files (Word, Excel, pictures) open in their own program. **Open in PDF app** in the Viewer's toolbar opens the same file in the PDF program set in Windows.

## Moving around

- The page strip on the left shows a small picture of every page. Click one to go to it; right-click it to delete or extract that page (see [page tools](help:page-tools)).
- **Previous page** and **Next page** step one page. Type a number in **Go to** and press Enter to jump. The toolbar shows **Page 3 of 12**.
- **Continuous** shows the pages one under another, so you can scroll through the whole document.
- **−** and **+** zoom out and in. **Fit width** fits the page to the window.
- **Rotate view** turns the pages on screen only; the file is not changed. Annotating is off while the view is turned: rotate it back first.

The footer shows the page count and the file size.

## Finding words

Type in **Find** and press Enter. The matches are marked on the pages and the toolbar says how many there are and on how many pages. **Previous match** and **Next match** go from one to the next. If the words are not there, a message says so.

To copy text, choose **Select text** on the **Annotate** panel and drag a box over the words; they go to the clipboard.

## Save and Save as

Annotations, stamps, pasted tapes and [form entries](help:forms) are not written into the file until you save. While some are waiting, the toolbar shows how many are **unsaved**, and the right-hand panel lists them under **Unsaved**, each with **Remove**, and **Discard all**. See [annotations](help:annotations).

- **Save** writes them into this file as a new version. The current version is kept and can be opened or restored from the document's details in Browse.
- **Save as…** writes a new document beside this one, with the same client, year and doc type. This file is not changed.

Both buttons are greyed out when nothing is waiting. On a digitally signed PDF, **Save** asks first, because saving changes into it makes the signature invalid (see [filling in forms](help:forms)).

## Closing

**Close** closes the document and takes you back to [Browse](help:browse). If changes are unsaved, Vibe Files asks first: **Keep editing** goes back so you can save them, **Discard and close** drops them. Opening another document while changes are unsaved asks the same question, with **Discard and open**. Closing Vibe Files itself with changes unsaved shows the document and asks: **Save and quit** saves them as a new version first, **Discard and quit** drops them, **Keep editing** stays.

> If the file was changed on another PC, by sync, or by restoring a version while you had it open, your unsaved edits are refused rather than put on the wrong pages. Note them, discard, and open the document again.

The toolbar also has **Print…** (see [printing](help:printing)), **Pages…**, **Flatten**, **PDF/A** and **Compress** (see [page tools](help:page-tools)). The ten-key tape is the **10-key** tab in the right-hand panel (see [the ten-key tape](help:ten-key)), and a fillable PDF is filled in on the **Form** tab (see [filling in forms](help:forms)).
